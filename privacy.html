Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2026

This policy explains what information the SkillBridge portal handles and how it is used. SkillBridge connects students, faculty, companies and institutions around skills, learning, internships and placements.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the portal, we may handle:

  • Contact details such as your name and email address
  • Academic details such as your institution, course and verified skills or certificates
  • Information you add to a portfolio, application or profile
  • Basic technical data such as browser type, needed to keep the portal working

2. How we use it

  • To show skill gaps and suggest learning paths
  • To match students with internships, jobs and projects
  • To let institutions verify student records
  • To improve and secure the portal

3. Sharing

Your profile is shared with a company only when you apply to that company or choose to share it. Institutions may see records of their own students. Reports for institutions and companies use combined data that does not identify individuals. We do not sell personal information.

4. Storage and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as your institution requires. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, though no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Your choices

You can ask to view, correct or delete the information held about you, and you can withdraw consent to share your profile at any time. Some academic records may need to be kept by your institution.

6. Cookies

The portal uses only what is needed to work. It does not use advertising trackers.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Questions

For questions or requests about your information, contact the team that runs SkillBridge at your institution.
