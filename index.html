    How it works

    The portal follows one path from skills to a job, so each step feeds the next.

    1. Assess

      Find out which technical and soft skills you have and where you fall short.

    2. Map

      Match your skills to job roles, industries and learning paths.

    3. Learn

      Take courses, workshops and training to close the gaps.

    4. Apply

      Apply to internships and jobs that fit the skills you have proven.

    What the portal offers

    Objective: one platform that links skills, learning, opportunities and placements, so colleges and companies work together more closely.

    Skill assessment and profiling

    See your technical and soft skills and the areas to improve.

    Skill mapping

    Match current skills to job roles, industries and learning paths.

    Learning and certification

    Industry-approved courses, workshops and training.

    Internship opportunities

    Companies post internships and projects and the portal suggests suitable students.

    Placement support

    Job recommendations and simple applications based on your skills.

    Digital student portfolio

    Verified skills, certificates, projects and internships in one shareable profile.

    Academician opportunities

    Industry training, research projects and consultancy work for faculty.

    Industry and academia collaboration

    Guest lectures, mentoring and live innovation challenges.

    Dashboards and analytics

    Data on student readiness, skill growth and what industry needs.

    Role-based access

    Separate dashboards for students, teachers, colleges and companies.

    Who it is for

    Each group gets its own set of tools.

    Student workspace

    • Take skill assessments
    • See gaps against target roles
    • Follow learning pathways
    • Apply to matched internships and jobs
    • Build a verified digital portfolio
    • Track application status

    Academician portal

    • Find faculty internships
    • Join industrial training and FDPs
    • Take up consultancy projects
    • Start research collaborations
    • Host guest lectures and workshops
    • Mentor student projects

    Industry dashboard

    • Post internships, jobs and apprenticeships
    • Get candidate recommendations
    • Shortlist by verified skills
    • Run innovation challenges
    • Offer mentorships and live projects
    • Track applications in one place

    Institution analytics

    • Monitor skill development by department
    • Watch internship participation
    • Measure placement readiness
    • See skill-demand trends
    • Approve and verify student records
    • Manage documents securely
