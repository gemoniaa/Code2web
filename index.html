Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

The SkillBridge Academia-Industry Collaboration Platform is committed to maintaining transparent data governance standards for all registered students, academicians, corporate partners, and institutional administrators.

1. Information Collection & Scope

We collect academic records, verified technical competencies, institutional email addresses, and professional contact details necessary to facilitate skill mapping, internship applications, and research collaboration.

2. Data Utilization

Student profiles are accessible to corporate recruiters only through explicit candidate consent or direct internship application submission. Institutional metrics are aggregated to protect individual privacy while delivering actionable skill-gap insights.

3. Verification & Security

All certification records and portfolio credentials undergo faculty verification prior to indexing. We enforce encryption and strict access protocols across all database instances.

Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Platform Usage & Eligibility

Access to SkillBridge is granted to enrolled students, authorized faculty members, registered corporate entities, and affiliated university coordinators. Users must provide accurate skill and credential data.

2. Intellectual Property & Research Collaborations

Joint research grants, consultancy projects, and faculty development engagements agreed upon via SkillBridge adhere to mutual institutional Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) guidelines.

3. Application Integrity

Submitting false certifications or fraudulent placement claims will result in immediate profile suspension and reporting to institutional academic ethics committees.
