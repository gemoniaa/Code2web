Privacy Policy

SkillBridge in this form has no server and no accounts. Everything you enter is stored only in your own browser (local storage) on this device.

  • What is stored: the name you enter, assessment results, courses, applications, portfolio entries, postings you create, and the file name, size and type of any document you add.
  • Documents you add are not read or uploaded. Only their names and sizes are kept.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies are used. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of that request.
  • You can delete everything at any time with the "Clear my data" button in the Access tab, or by clearing site data in your browser.

If this portal is deployed by an institution or company, that organisation is responsible for its own contact details and any server-side data handling it adds.
