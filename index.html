What the portal does

Objective: Build a unified platform that connects skills → learning → opportunities → placements, creating stronger collaboration between academia and industry.

Skill Assessment & Profiling

Find out what technical and soft skills you have, and see exactly where you need to improve.

Skill Mapping

Match your current skills directly to the right job roles, industries, and learning paths.

Learning & Certification

Take industry-approved courses, workshops, and training to build the skills you are missing.

Internship Opportunities

Companies post internships and projects, and the portal matches them with the right students.

Placement Support

Get job recommendations, apply easily, and get hired based on your actual skills.

Digital Student Portfolio

Keep all your verified skills, certificates, projects, and internships in one easy-to-share digital profile.

Academician Opportunities

Help teachers find industry training, research projects, and consultancy work.

Industry–Academia Collaboration

Bring colleges and companies together for guest lectures, mentoring, and live innovation challenges.

Dashboards & Analytics

Give colleges and companies clear data to track student readiness, skill growth, and what the industry needs.

Role-Based Access

Secure, separate dashboards with special tools just for students, teachers, colleges, and companies.
